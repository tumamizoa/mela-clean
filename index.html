What "mela clean" means

The Mela Clean Guarantee

In Maltese, "mela" is a highly versatile filler word. It means many things, but to us at Mela Clean, it is a guarantee of quality and safety you can count on.

Deep & detailed clean

Cleaning every nook, cranny, groove, edge, corner, and other areas that are often missed.

Eco-friendly

We use safe, non-toxic, and eco-friendly products engineered to match the cleaning power of industrial chemicals.

Safe & pet-friendly

Every product we use assures that your staff, occupants, pets, and the environment will not be harmed.

What "mela clean" means

The places a normal clean leaves behind.

A surface can look clean from across the room. Mela clean means going back over the bits that get skipped — the grooves, edges, and corners where everything else collects in commercial and common areas.

Tracks & grooves

Window and door tracks, shower screen rails in common bathrooms, and other channels that trap grit.

Grout & tile lines

Bathroom and kitchen grout scrubbed properly in shared spaces, not just wiped over.

Skirting boards & edges

The dust line along floors and behind doors that builds up in high-traffic areas.

Range hoods & vents

Filters, exhaust covers, and air vents that collect grease and dust over time.

Switches, handles & rails

Light switches, door handles, taps, and railings — high-touch surfaces cleaned properly.

Behind & under

Behind toilets, under equipment, and the spots that only get done when something is moved.

Air conditioning

Filters and vents wiped and cleared so the air moving through the space is cleaner too.

Windows & sills

Glass cleaned streak-free and sills wiped, including tracks where dust collects.

Plus everything else

Kitchens, bathrooms, floors, and surfaces — the full clean, done properly, every time.
